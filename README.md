## About

I build with AI and explore how GenAI, LLMs, RAG and Agentic AI can solve real problems. My interest is in the practical side: taking an idea, understanding the problem underneath it and turning it into something that works. I share what I am learning and building along the way.

I currently work at **314e**, and previously spent two years at **Tredence** building data and cloud solutions for production business problems. That Data Engineering background is what makes the AI work stick, because reliable AI applications are mostly a data and systems problem before they are a model problem.

---

## What I am working on

- Building practical AI applications with LLMs, RAG and the Model Context Protocol
- Exploring agentic systems and where autonomy is actually worth the complexity
- Retrieval quality: chunking, embeddings and evaluating what genuinely changes an answer
- Open source tooling for debugging RAG pipelines
- Writing about what works and what breaks
